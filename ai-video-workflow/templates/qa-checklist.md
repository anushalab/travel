# QA Checklist (run on every export)

## Visual
- [ ] Faces, hands and bodies have no warping/morphing
- [ ] Character, outfit and product identical across all shots
- [ ] No garbled text or fake logos in frame
- [ ] Consistent color grade between shots
- [ ] No flicker, jitter or sudden jumps inside a shot
- [ ] Resolution meets target (1080p minimum, 4K for hero)

## Story & pacing
- [ ] Strong visual hook in first 2 seconds
- [ ] Every shot earns its place; nothing over 5 s without motion
- [ ] CTA is clear and on screen long enough to read

## Audio
- [ ] VO clear, levels consistent (around -14 LUFS for social, -16 for web)
- [ ] Lip-sync accurate on presenter shots
- [ ] Music ducks under VO; no clipping
- [ ] Captions accurate and readable on mobile, inside safe zones

## Delivery
- [ ] All aspect-ratio cutdowns exported
- [ ] Thumbnail/cover created
- [ ] AI-generated content disclosure added where the platform requires it
- [ ] Rights: own/licensed music, consent for any real person's likeness or voice
